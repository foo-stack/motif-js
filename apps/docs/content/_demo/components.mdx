## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install motif-js">
    Add `motif-js` and `@motif-js/tokens` to your project via your favourite package manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a theme">
    Use `createTheme` to define a token tree. Both light and dark variants share the same shape.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wrap your app in a ThemeProvider">
    The provider emits a `<style>` block with all your CSS variables and applies the active theme by name.
  </Step>
</Steps>
