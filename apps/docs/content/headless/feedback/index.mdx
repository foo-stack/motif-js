## Why a family of one

Toast is the only behaviour here, but it is its own family because feedback is its own concern.
A toast is not an overlay, not a disclosure, not a form control - it is a notification that the
app raises, not the user. Grouping it alone keeps that distinction clear and leaves room for
future feedback behaviours.

The Toast page covers all three exports - the `Toaster` provider you mount once, the `useToast`
hook that pushes messages, and the `Toast` component that renders one with the right `aria-live`
semantics.
